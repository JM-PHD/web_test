Astrodynamics & Space Mission Analysis

We are developing novel approaches to astrodynamics, orbit modelling and space mission design with the aim of supporting future space missions to provide vital data and services needed on Earth, whilst preserving our fragile space environment for generations to come.

We welcome interest from potential PhD candidates enthusiastic about ensuring a responsible future for humanity's space activities.
