ad-ASSTRA: Towards A Sustainable Space Technology Roadmapping Architecture

As the number of spacecraft in orbit has risen dramatically, the sustainability of the space industry has come under increasing scrutiny. Space systems provide essential services, yet the rapid growth in launches is creating both orbital congestion and growing impacts on Earth.

This project focused on developing a longer-term, more integrated approach to sustainable space activity. Through a series of stakeholder workshops, we explored alternative future visions for the space sector and examined the complex links between orbital and terrestrial impacts. The work was carried out in collaboration with the Massachusetts Institute of Technology, establishing a lasting research partnership between the two institutions.

The project helped identify priority areas for future research and highlighted practical pathways for reducing unintended consequences while protecting the essential services that space provides.
