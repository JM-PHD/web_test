Visions of, and steps towards, an Environmentally Sustainable Space Future

Space systems deliver essential services such as climate monitoring, disaster response, and global communication and navigation. At the same time, our orbital environment is growing increasingly congested, and the rising number of launches is adding to impacts on Earth. Many current approaches remain short-term and reactive. A longer-term, more integrated perspective is needed if we are to protect the benefits of space while reducing unintended harm.

This project developed holistic assessment criteria for space missions, centred on the main environmental impact areas of the industry. Through a scenarios workshop, we brought together a range of stakeholders to explore different possible futures for the space sector. An integrated model was developed to characterise the key positive and negative impacts and their interdependencies. Potential mitigation options were evaluated against stakeholder perspectives, technical risk, and implementation complexity.
