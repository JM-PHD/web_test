Advancing Efficiency in Space Mission Design

Designing efficient space missions requires careful optimisation of performance, whether for Earth observation coverage, responsive manoeuvring over targets, or long-distance interplanetary travel. This work has developed new methods to support more effective mission design across these areas.

A key focus has been the creation of practical tools for identifying energy-efficient pathways through the Solar System. By mapping low-energy transfer opportunities and multi-planet flyby options, the work helps mission planners compare trajectories, improve flexibility, and reduce propulsion requirements.

Alongside the interplanetary analysis, approaches have also been explored for improving the efficiency of Earth observation missions, including better coverage assessment and responsive orbital manoeuvres. Together, these methods aim to support more flexible, lower-cost, and higher-performance mission designs for both near-Earth and deep-space applications.
